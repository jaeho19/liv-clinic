Most of the planning for a treatment trip to Seoul comes down to two things: how long it takes to get from the airport to the clinic, and whether the clinic is open when you get there. Miss the second one — land on a Saturday afternoon, say — and a two-day trip can lose its only usable slot.

This guide covers both for LIV Plastic Surgery (LIV), a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam. The clinic is one minute on foot from Sinsa Station (Line 3, Exit 4), next to Garosu-gil. Below are the address, the travel times from Incheon and Gimpo airports as the clinic publishes them, the opening hours in Korea time, and a way to line up your arrival with those hours.

## Address and nearest station

**Address:** 4F, Jaeun Building, 80 Naruteo-ro, Seocho-gu, Seoul, Korea

**Nearest station:** Sinsa Station, Seoul Subway Line 3, Exit 4 — a 1-minute walk. The clinic is on the 4th floor of the Jaeun Building.

**Neighbourhood:** the clinic is next to Garosu-gil, so the street is within walking distance before or after your appointment.

**Phone:** +82-2-797-2773 · **Email:** info@livps.co.kr

## Opening hours, in Korea time and in yours

The clinic's hours are given in Korea time (KST). Korea does not share a time zone with every market it serves, so check the column that applies to you before you propose an appointment time.

| Day | Korea time | Japan | Taiwan / Hong Kong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 10:00–19:00 | 10:00–19:00 (no difference) | 09:00–18:00 |
| Saturday | 10:00–16:00 | 10:00–16:00 (no difference) | 09:00–15:00 |
| Sunday | Closed | Closed | Closed |

Japan is on the same time as Korea. Taiwan and Hong Kong are one hour behind Korea, so a 10:00 appointment in Seoul is 09:00 on a phone still set to Taipei or Hong Kong time. If you are travelling from anywhere else, check the difference to Korea time yourself and confirm the appointment time in Korea time with the clinic.

The same applies to messages. Enquiries are answered within one business day, and "business day" follows the hours above — a message sent on Saturday evening or on Sunday is picked up when the clinic reopens.

The clinic is generally closed on Korean public holidays as well, including the Seollal and Chuseok holiday periods. If your trip overlaps a holiday, check the available days on chat before you book flights.

## Planning your arrival around clinic hours

Most non-surgical treatments take 30 to 90 minutes and need no hospitalization, so many patients are treated on the day of their visit. That makes an arrival-day appointment realistic — if the hours work. Count backwards from closing time: the journey from the airport, the consultation, any numbing time (about 30 minutes of anesthetic cream for Ultherapy Prime), and the treatment itself (for example 60–90 minutes for Ultherapy Prime, 45–60 for Thermage FLX, 10–20 for botox).

| You land | What usually works |
|---|---|
| Weekday morning | A same-day afternoon appointment. The clinic is open until 19:00. |
| Weekday afternoon or evening | An appointment the next morning from 10:00 is the safer choice. |
| Friday evening | Saturday, between 10:00 and 16:00. Book the Saturday slot early in the day. |
| Saturday morning | Possible only if you can reach Sinsa with time to spare before 16:00. Otherwise plan for Monday. |
| Saturday afternoon or Sunday | The clinic is closed on Sunday. The first available day is Monday from 10:00. |

Three habits make scheduling easier:

- **Send your flight times, not just your dates.** The clinic schedules around them.
- **Offer two or three possible slots** in your first message.
- **If your flight is delayed, say so through the same messenger as early as you can.** No deposit is required to reserve a consultation, so a change of plan is a matter of rescheduling.

## FAQ

### How do I get from Incheon Airport to the clinic?

Take the AREX airport train or a limousine bus to Sinsa Station on Subway Line 3. The clinic is a 1-minute walk from Exit 4. Public transport takes about 70–90 minutes, and a taxi takes about 60 minutes.

### How long does it take from Gimpo Airport?

About 40 minutes by taxi or subway. From Sinsa Station, use Exit 4 on Line 3; the clinic is a 1-minute walk away.

### Which exit of Sinsa Station is the clinic near?

Exit 4, on Line 3. The clinic is a 1-minute walk from the exit, on the 4th floor of the Jaeun Building, next to Garosu-gil.

### Is the clinic open on weekends?

On Saturday, from 10:00 to 16:00 Korea time. It is closed on Sunday, and generally on Korean public holidays. Weekday hours are 10:00 to 19:00.

### Is there a time difference I should allow for?

Clinic hours are in Korea time. Japan has no time difference with Korea. Taiwan and Hong Kong are one hour behind, so 10:00 in Seoul is 09:00 there. Always confirm your appointment in Korea time.
