Ultherapy and Thermage are the names most visitors already know. Once you start reading Korean clinic menus, three more appear again and again: Density, ONDA and InMode. This guide sets out how [LIV Plastic Surgery](/international) describes each of them, what they take in time and recovery, and what they cost, so you can ask better questions before you message a clinic. LIV is a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station and next to Garosu-gil. International patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients, and you can book directly in English without an agency.

## The short answer

The three do not use the same energy, and the clinic does not describe them for the same person.

- **Density** is described by the clinic as an entry-level RF (radiofrequency) lifting device at a reasonable cost. It covers the forehead, eye area, cheeks, jawline and neck.
- **ONDA** is not RF. It uses 2.45 GHz microwave energy, needs no anesthesia, and is the only one of the three the clinic also uses on the body.
- **InMode** is the option the clinic recommends when facial fat and sagging skin are a combined concern, with FX and FORMA modes priced separately.

This is not a ranking. Which one fits you, or whether a different treatment on the [lifting pages](/lifting) fits better, is decided with the doctor at the consultation.

## What each device is for

### Density

The clinic describes Density for people who want a combined lifting effect, who want both an immediate effect and long-term improvement, and who were not satisfied with an earlier lifting treatment. Treatment takes 40–60 minutes; anesthetic cream is optional. The clinic's results line reads "immediate lifting + collagen regeneration over 3–6 months", and it recommends an interval of 3–6 months between sessions. Asked how it differs from Ultherapy Prime and Thermage, the clinic's answer is that Density is an entry-level RF lifting device at a reasonable cost. See the [Density page](/lifting/density).

### ONDA

ONDA is often assumed to be another RF device. The clinic's own answer is no: it uses 2.45 GHz microwave energy. It is described for people who have loss of elasticity together with localized fat, who want improvement without downtime, and who want to care for the face and body together. On the face it treats the cheeks and jawline and the double chin in 15–30 minutes; on the body it treats the abdomen, flanks, thighs and arms at about 10 minutes per area. It is performed without anesthesia and is reported to feel similar to a warm massage. Results are not immediate: they appear gradually over 4–12 weeks and vary by individual. See the [ONDA page](/lifting/onda).

### InMode

The clinic recommends InMode for people whose concerns combine facial fat and sagging skin. Its listed uses are reducing fat under the chin while strengthening elasticity, lifting the cheeks and tidying the face line, improving a double chin and the deep cheek area, and collagen regeneration with improvement of fine lines. Treatment takes 30–60 minutes, with no anesthesia or with anesthetic cream. The clinic lists it alongside Shurink as a maintenance treatment that can be done every 3–6 months. It can also be combined with Thermage: the clinic says RF synergy allows a stronger improvement in elasticity. See the [InMode page](/lifting/inmode).

## What to tell the doctor

These cautions are the clinic's own. Mention anything that applies in your first message.

- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding.** Density and InMode are not performed during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. For ONDA, the clinic asks you to tell it at the consultation if you are pregnant or breastfeeding.
- **Pacemaker or metal implants.** For ONDA, tell the clinic if you have a pacemaker or a metal implant in the body.
- **Skin problems in the treatment area.** For ONDA, tell the clinic about any inflammation, infection or wound in the area. For Density and InMode, whether treatment is possible depends on your skin condition and is checked at the consultation.
- **Underlying conditions.** For ONDA, tell the clinic about conditions such as uncontrolled diabetes.
- **After the treatment.** Mild swelling or redness is possible after Density and InMode.

## FAQ

### Is ONDA a radiofrequency (RF) treatment?

No. ONDA uses 2.45 GHz microwave energy. Density and InMode are the RF options in this comparison.

### Does Density, ONDA or InMode need anesthesia?

ONDA is performed without anesthesia and is reported to feel similar to a warm massage. InMode is done with no anesthesia or with topical anesthetic cream, and for Density the anesthetic cream is optional.

### How soon do results appear?

The clinic describes Density as immediate lifting plus collagen regeneration over 3–6 months, and InMode as immediate firmness plus gradual collagen regeneration. ONDA is not immediate: results appear gradually over 4–12 weeks. Results vary from person to person.

### How often are these treatments repeated?

The clinic recommends an interval of 3–6 months for Density, and lists InMode as a maintenance treatment that can be done every 3–6 months. The cycle depends on your skin condition and the result you want, so the exact frequency is set at the consultation.

### Can InMode be combined with Thermage?

Yes. The clinic says the RF synergy allows a stronger improvement in elasticity. Whether a combination suits you is decided at the consultation.
