A price list tells you what a treatment starts at. It does not tell you what ends up on the receipt — whether tax is included, whether a consultation is charged, whether there is a deposit, or whether a foreign patient pays something extra. This guide answers those questions for [LIV Plastic Surgery](/international), a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station, using only what the clinic publishes. In short: the same list applies to everyone, prices are per session in Korean won and exclude VAT, and the exact figure is confirmed at your consultation.

## The short version

- **One price list for everyone.** International patients pay from the same list as Korean patients. There is no foreigner surcharge.
- **Prices are per session, in KRW, and exclude VAT.** VAT is added on top of the listed price.
- **The tilde (~) means "from".** The exact price is determined after consultation.
- **Consultation fee: KRW 10,000,** fully deducted from the treatment cost if you have treatment the same day.
- **Interpretation is free,** and there is no extra charge for language support.
- **No deposit** is required to reserve a consultation.
- **Payment:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB, UnionPay, or Korean won in cash. Card installments are available.

## How to read the public price list

The [pricing page](/pricing) publishes four groups — thread lift, filler, Thermage and Ultherapy — and each treatment page carries its own table (for example [Botox](/antiaging/botox), [skin boosters](/antiaging/skinbooster) and the [laser pages](/laser)). Every row has the same three parts: an item, a basis, and a price.

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| KRW 1,560,000~ | A starting price in Korean won. The tilde means "from". |
| A basis such as "400-800 shots", "1cc", "8 threads", "per 100IU", "single session" | What the price covers. The amount you actually need is set at the consultation. |
| "Per session" | One treatment visit, not a course. |
| "All prices are exclusive of VAT" | VAT is added to the listed price when you pay. |
| "Detailed pricing provided during consultation" | The item is not priced publicly; you get a quote in person or by message. |

Some rows as they appear on the clinic's lists:

## What is added to the price — and what is not

| Item | What you pay | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment | The listed price, confirmed at consultation | Per session, in KRW |
| VAT | Added on top of the treatment price | The list is VAT-exclusive |
| 1:1 consultation | KRW 10,000 | Fully deducted from the treatment cost if you proceed the same day |
| Interpretation (English, Japanese, Chinese) | Free | Arranged on request |
| Foreign-patient surcharge | None | Same price list as Korean patients |
| Deposit to reserve a consultation | None | You pay at the clinic |

Because the list excludes VAT, ask for the VAT-inclusive total when you receive your estimate. That single number is the easiest way to compare clinics.

## Payment methods and installments

- **Cards issued abroad:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB and UnionPay are accepted.
- **Cash:** Korean won (KRW).
- **Installments:** credit card installment payments are available. Interest-free installment terms vary by card company and payment amount, so ask during your consultation. [검수 필요: 해외 발급 카드도 할부 결제가 되는지]
- **Currency:** prices are set in Korean won. If you pay by card, the amount that appears on your statement depends on the exchange rate applied on the day.
- **When you pay:** at the clinic. No deposit is taken to reserve a consultation; if your plans change, let the clinic know through the same messenger as early as you can.

If you have a specific question about payment, the clinic asks you to raise it during the consultation — or simply include it in your first message.

## What is decided at the consultation

The estimate you receive by messenger is based on photos. The in-person consultation with the board-certified plastic surgeon is where the plan and the price become final:

- **Which treatment,** and whether one treatment or a combination suits your concern.
- **How much:** the number of shots for [Ultherapy](/lifting/ulthera) or [Thermage](/lifting/thermage), cc for [filler](/antiaging/filler), units and product for [Botox](/antiaging/botox), the number of threads, or the number of laser sessions.
- **Which product,** where the list has tiers such as domestic, domestic premium and imported.
- **The final price,** including VAT.

You are free to have the consultation and decide later. The clinic says so directly: you can take your time to decide after the consultation.

## FAQ

### Do foreigners pay more than Korean patients?

No. Foreign patients are charged from the same price list as Korean patients, and the prices are published so you can check them before your visit. There is also no extra charge for language support or interpretation.

### Is VAT included in the listed prices?

No. All listed prices are exclusive of VAT, which is added when you pay. Ask the clinic for the VAT-inclusive total when you receive your estimate.

### Is there a consultation fee, and do I get it back?

The 1:1 consultation costs KRW 10,000. If you proceed with treatment on the same day, it is fully deducted from the treatment cost. You can also have only the consultation and decide later.

### Do I need to pay a deposit to book?

No. No deposit is required to reserve a consultation. You pay at the clinic.

### Can I pay with a foreign credit card or in installments?

Internationally issued Visa, Mastercard, American Express, JCB and UnionPay cards are accepted, as is cash in Korean won. Card installment payments are available; interest-free terms vary by card company and amount, so ask at the consultation.
