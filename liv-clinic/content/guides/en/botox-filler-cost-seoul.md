## How to read the prices

- **The tilde means "from".** The final price is set at your in-person consultation once the surgeon has agreed the area and the amount with you, and VAT is added on top.
- **Botox: the product is what moves the price.** The same contour area runs from KRW 60,000 with a domestic product to KRW 190,000 with Allergan. Products differ in onset time, diffusion and duration, which is why the choice is made at the consultation rather than online.
- **Body Botox is priced per 100 IU;** the dose for each area is decided by the surgeon at the consultation, after looking at the area in person.
- **Filler is priced per cc:** the per-cc price times the number of cc used. How many cc an area takes varies from person to person.
- **Nothing is added for being a foreign patient,** and there is no fee for interpretation or language support.

Practical notes from the clinic's own pages:

- After Botox, avoid alcohol and saunas on the day of treatment, and do not massage the treated area.
- After filler, bruising or swelling is possible; in general it settles within a few days to about a week, although this varies from person to person. Do not massage or press the area, and avoid strenuous exercise and saunas for 3 days.
- The clinic notes that a Botox touch-up may be needed 2–3 weeks after a first treatment. If you will have left Korea by then, say so at the consultation: the clinic can review your progress from photos sent by messenger, then either adjust at your next visit or advise you on what to do locally. Ask about the cost of a touch-up at the consultation.
- Flying home: neither treatment needs a follow-up visit, and as non-surgical treatments they generally place no restriction on flying, so a flight the same day or the next day is usually not a problem. Confirm the timing for your own case at the consultation.

## Who these treatments suit, and what to tell the clinic

The clinic describes [Botox](/antiaging/botox) as suited to people concerned about expression wrinkles, those who want to reduce masseter (jaw muscle) or trapezius volume, those who want a quick and simple treatment, and younger patients interested in preventive anti-aging. Areas named on the site: forehead, glabella, eye area, masseter, mouth corners, trapezius and calves.

[Dermal filler](/antiaging/filler) suits people who want to fill hollowed areas, soften nasolabial folds or lines around the mouth, enhance the nose or chin contour, or have fuller lips. Areas named: forehead, temples, nose, cheekbones, nasolabial folds, cheeks, chin, aegyo-sal (the band under the eyes), lips and brows.

Points to raise at your consultation:

- Botox and filler are not recommended during pregnancy or while breastfeeding; the clinic advises waiting until both are complete.
- Botox is not performed for people with neuromuscular disorders.
- Filler carries a rare risk of vascular occlusion, which is why the clinic stresses treatment by experienced medical staff. If anything feels wrong after treatment, contact the clinic straight away.
- If skin quality rather than volume is your concern, [skin boosters](/antiaging/skinbooster) are a separate treatment with their own price list.

## FAQ

### Do foreigners pay more for Botox or filler in Korea?

Not at LIV. International patients are charged from the same price list as Korean patients, published on the [pricing page](/pricing) so you can check it before you travel. Prices are per session and exclude VAT, and interpretation costs nothing extra.

### Will Botox make my face look frozen?

The clinic's answer is that when the proper amount is injected at the correct locations, natural expressions are maintained while only the wrinkles improve; unnatural results usually come from overdosing or incorrect injection sites. The dose is tailored to your muscle characteristics at the consultation.

### How long do Botox and filler last?

Botox results begin 3–7 days after treatment and typically last 3–6 months, varying by individual; with regular treatments the interval between sessions can lengthen. Filler results are immediate and last 6–24 months depending on the product.

### Can filler be dissolved if I don't like it?

Yes. Hyaluronic acid filler can be dissolved with hyaluronidase if you are unhappy with the result or a side effect occurs. Non-reversible fillers cannot be dissolved, so ask which type is being used. Occasional unevenness can usually be resolved by the clinic with massage or, if necessary, hyaluronidase.

### Can I have Botox or filler on the same day as my consultation?

In most cases, yes. Botox takes 10–20 minutes and filler 20–40 minutes, neither requires hospitalization, and neither needs a return visit, so one appointment is usually all you need to plan.

### Is there a consultation fee?

Yes: KRW 10,000 for the 1:1 consultation with the surgeon, fully deducted from the treatment cost if you have treatment the same day. You are welcome to consult only and decide later.
