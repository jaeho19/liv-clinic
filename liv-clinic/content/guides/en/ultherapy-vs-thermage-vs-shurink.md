Three names keep coming up when you read about lifting treatments in Seoul: Ultherapy, Thermage and Shurink. This guide is for people who want to work out which is right for them before messaging a clinic, and to see how a Seoul clinic actually makes that call. [LIV Plastic Surgery](/international) is a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station and next to Garosu-gil. International patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients, and you can book directly in English without an agency.

## The short answer

The three differ first in the type of energy they use and the layer of skin they work on. Ultherapy Prime uses HIFU (high-intensity focused ultrasound) to deliver energy deep into the skin's SMAS layer. Thermage FLX uses RF (radiofrequency) energy to contract collagen in the dermal layer and promote its regeneration. Shurink is also HIFU and, in the clinic's words, suits regular care at a reasonable cost.

In the way LIV describes them on its [lifting pages](/lifting): Ultherapy Prime for lifting sagging skin, Thermage for overall skin elasticity, and Shurink for a first lifting treatment, a quick session or regular maintenance. Ultherapy and Thermage can also be combined, which the clinic says can produce synergistic effects. None of this is a ranking: which fits you, or whether two should be combined, is decided at the consultation from your skin condition and goals.

## What each treatment is for

### Ultherapy Prime

The HIFU treatment the clinic describes for people who want lifting without surgery, are concerned about sagging skin or loss of elasticity, prefer a natural-looking change and want no downtime. Areas treated: forehead, eye area, cheeks, jawline and neck. Anesthetic cream goes on for about 30 minutes, then the treatment takes 60–90 minutes. Results typically last 1–2 years, though this varies from person to person, and the clinic recommends it once every 1–2 years. LIV is an officially certified Ultherapy Prime provider; see the [Ultherapy page](/lifting/ulthera) and our [Ultherapy price guide](/guides/ultherapy-cost-seoul).

### Thermage FLX

The RF option, described for people concerned about loss of elasticity or fine lines, who want a natural, gradual change and prefer a treatment without anesthesia. It treats the full face, eye area, neck and body, with a dedicated Thermage Eye tip around the eyes. Compared with earlier versions, FLX's AccuREP technology auto-tunes the energy and the treatment is 25% faster. The clinic recommends 1–2 sessions per year. LIV is a Thermage FLX partner clinic; see the [Thermage page](/lifting/thermage).

### Shurink

Also HIFU. The clinic positions it for people having a lifting treatment for the first time, who want a quick session, lifting at a reasonable cost, or regular upkeep. Treatment takes 30–45 minutes, anesthetic cream is optional, and the areas are the forehead, eye area, cheeks, jawline and neck. It is repeated every 3–6 months and also suits maintenance care. See the [Shurink page](/lifting/shurink).

## How LIV decides between them

1. **Remote consultation first.** Send your concerns and a few photos by WhatsApp or the live chat on this site; the clinic suggests suitable treatments and sends a clear price estimate before you travel.
2. **In-person diagnosis by the surgeon.** At the clinic, the board-certified plastic surgeon diagnoses your skin and recommends a plan based on your concerns and the result you want. Every treatment is planned and performed under Dr. Sooyoung Kim, whose listed specialties include Ultherapy Prime lifting, Thermage lifting and combination lifting treatments; see [our medical team](/about/staff).
3. **One treatment, or two together.** Because Ultherapy Prime is described for lifting and Thermage for overall elasticity, the clinic may propose one or both. Shurink may be proposed where a quick session, lifting at a reasonable cost or regular maintenance is the goal.
4. **The frequency follows the choice.** Ultherapy Prime is recommended once every 1–2 years, Thermage once every 6 months to a year, and maintenance treatments such as Shurink every 3–6 months; the exact cycle is set at the consultation.
5. **You can take your time.** The 1:1 consultation fee is KRW 10,000, fully deducted from the treatment cost if you go ahead the same day. You are free to decide later.

## What to tell the surgeon

These cautions are the clinic's own; raise them in your first message so the right option is proposed from the start.

- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding.** None of the three treatments is performed during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
- **Pacemaker.** Thermage FLX is not performed on people with a pacemaker.
- **Metal implants.** For Ultherapy Prime and Thermage FLX, a metal implant in the treatment area needs a consultation first.
- **Sensitive skin.** For Shurink, sensitive skin needs a consultation first.
- **After the treatment.** Mild swelling or redness is possible after Ultherapy Prime, temporary redness after Thermage FLX and mild redness after Shurink; depending on the area, Ultherapy can cause a temporary change in sensation. Mild swelling and redness typically settle within a few hours to 1–2 days, though this varies from person to person.

## FAQ

### What is the difference between Ultherapy and Thermage?

Ultherapy Prime uses HIFU (high-intensity focused ultrasound) to deliver energy deep into the skin's SMAS layer, while Thermage uses RF (radiofrequency) energy to contract collagen in the dermal layer and promote its regeneration. The clinic describes Ultherapy Prime as effective for lifting sagging skin and Thermage as effective for overall skin elasticity.

### Can Ultherapy and Thermage be combined?

Yes. The clinic says combining the two can produce synergistic effects, and combination lifting treatments are among the surgeon's listed specialties. Whether a combination suits you is decided at the consultation.

### Do international patients pay more for these treatments in Korea?

### Can I have the treatment on the same day as the consultation?

In most cases, yes. All three take 30–90 minutes with no hospitalization, and Ultherapy Prime and Thermage FLX need no revisit; whether a Shurink follow-up is wanted is confirmed at the consultation.

### How do I know the devices and tips are genuine?

### Is there a consultation fee?

The 1:1 consultation fee is KRW 10,000, fully deducted from the treatment cost if you proceed with treatment on the same day. You can also have the consultation only and decide later.
