### What the price covers

- **The shot count for your area.** Ultherapy is priced by the number of shots (pulses of focused ultrasound) delivered. The upper face means roughly 200–300 shots, a full face 400–800, and adding the neck starts from 600 shots. The surgeon decides the count at your consultation.
- **A genuine Ultherapy Prime system and authentic cartridges.** LIV is an officially certified Ultherapy Prime provider and a Thermage FLX partner clinic. Only genuine, certified handpieces (tips) are used, and you are welcome to check this yourself before treatment.
- **Language support at no extra charge.** Consultations are available in English, and interpretation is arranged free of charge on request. There is no foreigner surcharge and no fee for language support.
- **An in-person consultation with the surgeon** before the treatment, on the same visit. [검수 필요: 상담비 유무 — 사이트에 근거 없음]

## How long it takes, and whether you need to come back

- **Time at the clinic.** A topical anesthetic cream is applied for about 30 minutes, then the treatment itself takes 60–90 minutes. Most non-surgical treatments at LIV take 30 to 90 minutes and need no hospitalization, so many patients are treated on the same day as their visit.
- **Return visit.** Ultherapy Prime is completed in one session: same day, no revisit required. That is what makes it easy to fit into a short trip.
- **Downtime.** You can return to daily life immediately. Mild swelling or redness is possible after the treatment, and depending on the area a temporary change in sensation can occur. [검수 필요: 붓기·홍조가 가라앉는 일수 — 사이트에 근거 없음]
- **Pain.** With the topical anesthetic, most people tolerate the treatment well.
- **When you see results.** There is an immediate effect, then gradual improvement over 3–6 months. Results last 1–2 years.

## Is Ultherapy Prime right for you?

The clinic describes Ultherapy Prime as suited to people who:

- want lifting without surgery;
- are concerned about sagging skin or loss of elasticity;
- prefer a natural-looking change; and
- want a treatment without downtime.

Areas treated are the forehead, eye area, cheeks, jawline and neck.

Points to raise at your consultation:

- Ultherapy is not performed during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
- If you have a metal implant in the treatment area, a consultation is needed first.
- Mild swelling or redness, and a temporary change in sensation in the treated area, are possible.

If you are weighing it against other lifting options at LIV: Ultherapy uses focused ultrasound (HIFU), while [Thermage FLX](/lifting/thermage) uses radiofrequency (RF). [Shurink](/lifting/shurink) is also HIFU and, in the clinic's words, suits regular care at a reasonable cost. Which one fits you is decided at the consultation.

## FAQ

### Do international patients pay more for Ultherapy in Korea?

No. At LIV, international patients are charged from the same price list as Korean patients, published on the [pricing page](/pricing). Prices are per session and exclude VAT, and there is no extra charge for interpretation.

### How many shots will I need?

It depends on the area: roughly 200–300 shots for the upper face, 300–400 for the lower face, 400–800 for the full face, and from 600 shots for the full face and neck. The surgeon sets the exact count at your in-person consultation, which is also when the final price is confirmed.

### Does Ultherapy hurt?

The treatment is done after a topical anesthetic cream has been on for about 30 minutes, and most people tolerate it well.

### Can I have the treatment on the same day as my consultation?

In most cases, yes. The treatment takes 60–90 minutes, requires no hospitalization, and Ultherapy Prime needs no revisit, so one appointment is usually all you need to plan.

### When will I see results, and how long do they last?

There is an immediate effect, followed by gradual improvement over 3–6 months. Results last 1–2 years.

### How do I know the clinic uses a genuine Ultherapy device?

LIV is an officially certified Ultherapy Prime provider and uses only genuine, certified handpieces (tips). You can ask to verify this before your treatment.
