A thread lift is the one lifting treatment at LIV that comes with a real recovery period, so it needs more planning than a device treatment you walk out of. This guide explains how the clinic describes its [thread lift](/lifting/thread) and its [APTOS thread lift](/lifting/aptos), what each thread costs, and how the 3–7 day recovery and the optional one-week check affect the length of your trip. [LIV Plastic Surgery](/international) is a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station and next to Garosu-gil. International patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients, and you can book directly in English without an agency.

## Thread lift and APTOS: how the clinic describes each

Both treatments place threads under local anesthesia, take 30–60 minutes and treat the same areas: forehead, cheekbones, cheeks, jawline, nasolabial folds and neck. The difference the site draws is in how long the result is expected to last and who each one is aimed at.

| | Thread lift | APTOS Bio Lifting |
|---|---|---|
| Treatment time | 30–60 minutes | 30–60 minutes |
| Anesthesia | Local anesthesia | Local anesthesia |
| Recovery | 3–7 days (bruising and swelling possible) | 3–7 days (bruising and swelling possible) |
| Results as stated | Immediate lifting, lasting 6–12 months | Immediate lifting, lasting up to 24 months |
| Combining with laser lifting | Possible after 1–2 weeks | Possible after 2–4 weeks |

**Thread lift.** The clinic describes it as suited to people who want immediate lifting, who are concerned about sagging cheeks or a flattened cheekbone area, who want to tidy the nasolabial folds and jawline, or who have not been satisfied with laser lifting alone. Results last 6–12 months depending on the type of thread.

**APTOS.** The clinic describes it as suited to people who want longer-lasting lifting, who want a biostimulation effect, who prefer a globally certified product, or who want natural-looking volume and elasticity. According to the APTOS page, APTOS NAMICA threads encapsulate 40 mg of hyaluronic acid and the effect lasts up to 24 months. The page lists the product's certifications as KFDA Class 4, CE, ISO 13485 and FDA MDSAP.

On duration in general, the clinic's medical Q&A adds that it depends on the thread material: PDO (dissolvable) threads last about 6–8 months and PLLA or PCL threads about 12–18 months, and because threads promote collagen regeneration as they dissolve, the effect continues for some time after they are absorbed. These are the clinic's stated ranges, not a promise for any individual; which thread suits your face is decided at the consultation.

## Downtime and aftercare

The clinic's stated recovery period for both treatments is 3–7 days, during which bruising and swelling are possible. Its aftercare points are:

- **Bruising and swelling** may appear for 3–7 days after the treatment.
- **Avoid exaggerated facial expressions and facial massage for 2 weeks.**
- **A pulling or tight sensation** in the treated area is possible.
- **Light makeup** is possible from 24 hours after the treatment.
- **Rare risks** the clinic lists are infection and thread exposure. If anything worries you after treatment, contact the clinic through the same chat you booked with.

Most cosmetic treatments, including lifting treatments, are not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Tell the surgeon about your medical history and any medication at the consultation.

## Follow-up: the one-week check is optional

For a thread lift, the clinic's international page lists an optional check about one week later, or a remote photo check instead. The medical Q&A puts it the same way: a check-up after one week is recommended if needed, but it is not required.

After you return home, you can send photos by messenger or chat and the clinic reviews your progress and guides your aftercare. A written aftercare guide is provided in English.

## How long to stay in Seoul

The treatment itself takes 30–60 minutes, needs no hospitalization, and in most cases is done on the same day as your in-person consultation. What decides the length of your trip is how you want to handle recovery and follow-up.

| Plan | What it looks like |
|---|---|
| Short stay with remote follow-up | Consultation and treatment on one visit, then the one-week check is done by photo after you are home. The 3–7 day bruising and swelling period will overlap with your return journey. |
| About one week | Treatment early in the trip, in-person check about one week later before you leave. Most of the 3–7 day recovery period passes while you are still in Seoul. |

## FAQ

### What is the difference between a thread lift and APTOS at LIV?

Both are done under local anesthesia in 30–60 minutes with a 3–7 day recovery period. The clinic states that a thread lift gives immediate lifting lasting 6–12 months, and that APTOS gives immediate lifting lasting up to 24 months; APTOS NAMICA threads encapsulate 40 mg of hyaluronic acid. Which one suits you is decided at the consultation.

### How much does a thread lift cost in Seoul at LIV?

Published starting prices per session are KRW 800,000 for MINT (8 threads), KRW 1,000,000 for Silhouette Soft (2 threads), KRW 1,500,000 for APTOS Light Lift 25 (2 threads), KRW 2,500,000 for APTOS Light Lift 50 (2 threads) and KRW 3,000,000 for APTOS NAMICA (10 threads). VAT is not included, and international patients pay from the same price list.

### How long is the downtime after a thread lift?

The clinic states 3–7 days, with bruising and swelling possible. Light makeup is possible from 24 hours after the treatment, and exaggerated facial expressions and massage should be avoided for 2 weeks.

### Do I have to come back for a check-up?

No. A check about one week later is optional, and it can be replaced by a remote photo check through messenger or chat after you return home.

### How many days should I stay in Seoul for a thread lift?

The treatment takes 30–60 minutes and needs no hospitalization. If you want the in-person check, plan for about one week; otherwise the check can be done by photo. The right timing for your return flight is confirmed with the clinic at your consultation.
