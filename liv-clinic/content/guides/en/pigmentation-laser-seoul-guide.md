Laser treatment for dark spots and melasma is high on many visitors' lists for Seoul, and it is also the treatment most likely to be misunderstood before a trip: pigmentation is usually treated over several sessions spaced weeks apart, not in a single afternoon. This guide lays out what [LIV Plastic Surgery](/international) publishes about its [laser](/laser) pigmentation care — the types of pigment it treats, the devices, the session counts and intervals, the prices that are listed, and the sun care that follows — so you can decide what is realistic for the days you have. LIV is a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station, and international patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients.

## Which kinds of pigmentation the clinic treats

The clinic's pigmentation page covers melasma, blemishes and spots, freckles and age spots. It describes the treatment as suited to people who:

- have melasma that is getting darker and wider
- have had multiple toning sessions without results
- are concerned about many spots and freckles
- are starting to develop age spots
- have a dull and uneven skin tone
- want consistent care without downtime

Different pigment sits at different depths and behaves differently, which is why the type is diagnosed in person before a device is chosen. Photos sent ahead help the clinic give you a first estimate, but the plan is set at the consultation with the surgeon.

## The three-stage system and the devices

LIV describes its approach as a three-stage pigmentation system, moving from intensive treatment to maintenance. Device names are given as the clinic writes them.

| Stage | Purpose | Device | What the clinic says it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stage 1 | Intensive treatment | Lucas Pico | Deep pigment breakdown with a picosecond pulse |
| Stage 2 | Precision targeting | Clarity II 755nm | Precise treatment of shallow pigment with the alexandrite wavelength |
| Stage 3 | Maintenance | Toning + Ulblanc | Gentle repeated treatment to maintain the effect and help prevent recurrence |

On the question many people ask — pico laser or toning? — the clinic's answer is that it depends on the goal. Stubborn melasma or deep pigmentation calls for intensive pico laser treatment, while mild pigmentation or maintenance suits toning; often a combination of pico treatment followed by toning maintenance is used. The page explains the picosecond pulse as 1,000 times faster than a nanosecond laser, breaking pigment into finer particles.

## Time, anesthesia and downtime by device

| Device | Treatment time | Anesthesia | Downtime |
|---|---|---|---|
| Laser Toning | 5–15 minutes | None | None; back to daily life immediately |
| Ulblanc | 15–20 minutes | None | None; back to daily life immediately |
| Clarity II | 15–30 minutes | None (cryogen cooling to minimise discomfort) | 3–5 days; fine scabs possible on pigmented areas |
| Lucas laser | 20–40 minutes | Topical anesthetic cream (optional) | 3–7 days; fine scabs possible |

After surface pigment is treated with Clarity II, the pigment may look darker or thin scabs may form for about 3–5 days before coming away naturally. If your trip includes photos or events, plan those days accordingly.

## Sun care and aftercare

Sun protection is the part of pigmentation treatment that happens after you leave the clinic, and the clinic treats it as essential:

- Skin is more sensitive after laser treatment, and the risk of pigmentation from UV exposure increases.
- For at least 2–4 weeks, apply sunscreen (SPF 50+) thoroughly and use a hat or a parasol.
- The clinic advises avoiding laser treatment during seasons when you will be outdoors a lot. If your Seoul trip is followed directly by a beach holiday or a ski trip, say so at the consultation.
- Temporary post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) can occur after laser. The clinic states that it improves naturally within 2–4 weeks and that thorough sun protection helps prevent it.

You receive a written aftercare guide in English, and after you return home the clinic can review photos sent by messenger or chat.

## What is realistic in one short trip

The honest point: the published programs run from 3 to 10 sessions at intervals of 2 to 4 weeks. A visit of a few days covers one session of such a program, not the program.

- **One session on this trip.** You can have a consultation and, in most cases, a session on the same day. Think of it as the first step of a plan, and ask the surgeon what to expect from a single session for your type of pigment.
- **Repeat visits.** If you travel to Seoul every few weeks or months, sessions can be spaced to the intervals above. Tell the clinic your travel pattern in your first message.
- **A longer stay.** A stay of a month or more allows more than one session at the 2-week or 3–4 week intervals.
- **Melasma is managed, not cured.** The clinic says melasma should be approached as management rather than cure: intensive treatment first, then regular maintenance and sun protection to minimise recurrence. That is hard to complete in one trip, and it is better to know this before you book.

If a single visit is all you have, the surgeon may suggest a different plan at the consultation. For timing around flights, see [downtime and flight planning](/guides/downtime-flight-travel-plan).

## FAQ

### Can melasma be completely cured with laser?

The clinic's answer is that melasma should be approached as management rather than cure. After improvement with intensive treatment, regular maintenance and sun protection are used to minimise recurrence, and LIV's three-stage system includes both treatment and maintenance.

### How many sessions does pigmentation laser take?

By the clinic's published protocols: 5–10 sessions of laser toning at 2-week intervals for mild pigmentation, 3–5 sessions of Clarity II 755nm at 3–4 week intervals for moderate pigmentation, and 5–10 sessions of Lucas Pico plus toning at 2–4 week intervals for deep or stubborn melasma. Your own number is set at the consultation.

### Can pigmentation get darker after laser?

Temporary post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation (PIH) can occur. The clinic states that it improves naturally within 2–4 weeks and can be prevented with thorough sun protection, and it gives aftercare guidance after treatment.

### How much does pigmentation laser cost in Seoul?

At LIV, a single session of Dual Toning Laser (Clarity + Lucas) is listed from KRW 150,000, and CO2 laser for moles and age spots from KRW 10,000 per spot of 3 mm or less. Prices are per session, exclude VAT, and are the same for international patients.

### Can I have pigmentation laser while pregnant?

No. The clinic does not recommend laser treatment during pregnancy or breastfeeding and advises treatment after both are complete. During pregnancy it suggests focusing on sun protection and moisturising.
