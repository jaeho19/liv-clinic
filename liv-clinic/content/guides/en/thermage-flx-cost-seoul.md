If you are sensitive to pain, additional pain control is available; mention it at your consultation.
- **Downtime.** You can return to daily life immediately. Temporary flushing is possible after the treatment.

- **How often.** The clinic recommends 1–2 sessions per year. The interval that suits you is set at the consultation.
- **Thermage FLX compared with earlier versions.** According to the clinic, AccuREP technology adjusts the energy automatically and the treatment is 25% faster.

## Is Thermage FLX right for you?

The clinic describes Thermage FLX as suited to people who:

- are concerned about loss of skin elasticity;
- want to improve fine lines;
- prefer a natural, gradual change; and
- want a treatment without anesthesia.

Points to raise at your consultation:

- Thermage FLX cannot be performed on people with a pacemaker.
- It is not performed during pregnancy or while breastfeeding.
- If you have a metal implant in the treatment area, a consultation is needed first.
- Temporary flushing is possible after the treatment.

If you are weighing it against Ultherapy: Thermage uses radiofrequency (RF) energy to contract collagen in the dermal layer and promote its regeneration, and the clinic describes it as effective for overall skin elasticity. [Ultherapy Prime](/lifting/ulthera) uses focused ultrasound (HIFU) that reaches the deeper SMAS layer, and is described as effective for lifting sagging skin. The two can be combined. The [Ultherapy vs Thermage vs Shurink guide](/guides/ultherapy-vs-thermage-vs-shurink) compares them in more detail, and the [Ultherapy price guide](/guides/ultherapy-cost-seoul) lists Ultherapy prices by area. Which one fits you is decided at the consultation.

## FAQ

### How much does Thermage FLX cost in Seoul at LIV?

The published starting prices per session are KRW 1,300,000 for 300 shots, KRW 2,400,000 for 600 shots and KRW 3,300,000 for 900 shots. ### Do international patients pay more?

No. At LIV, international patients are charged from the same price list as Korean patients, published on the [pricing page](/pricing). There is no extra charge for interpretation or language support.

### Can I have Thermage FLX on the same day as my consultation, and do I need to come back?

In most cases the treatment is done on the day of your visit. It takes 45–60 minutes, requires no hospitalization, and Thermage FLX is completed in one session with no revisit required.

### Does Thermage FLX hurt?

Pain varies with the treatment and the individual. Thermage FLX uses a vibrating tip that reduces discomfort, and the clinic's medical Q&A says most patients can receive the treatment comfortably. Additional pain control is available if you ask at your consultation.

### How long do Thermage results last, and how often should I repeat it?

The clinic states that Thermage results last about 6 months to 1 year, depending on skin condition, age and lifestyle, and recommends 1–2 sessions per year. Your own interval is decided with the surgeon.

### Can Thermage be used around the eyes?

Yes. The eye area is treated with a dedicated Thermage eye tip, listed at 225 or 450 shots.
