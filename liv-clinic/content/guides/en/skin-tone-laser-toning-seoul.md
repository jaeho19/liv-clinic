LIV is a non-surgical anti-aging clinic in Gangnam, one minute from Sinsa Station and next to Garosu-gil, and international patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients.

## What laser toning is

On the clinic's [laser pages](/laser), laser toning is described as a 1064nm Nd:YAG laser used at low power with repeated pulses, so that melanin in the skin is broken down gradually. Unlike conventional high-power lasers, it delivers low energy evenly over a wide area, which is why irritation is kept low and there is no recovery period.

The page lists three effects: gradual breakdown of pigment, stimulation of the dermis (collagen production), and control of sebaceous gland function with tightening of pores. The key word is *gradual* — this is a treatment built on repeated sessions, not a one-time procedure.

## Fitting a session into a short trip

What the facts support, plainly:

- **One session fits easily.** Laser toning and Ulblanc need no anesthesia and no recovery period, and the page describes returning to daily activities immediately. A session takes well under an hour.
- **One session is one step of a course.** The program is built on weekly or two-weekly sessions, and the clinic says a difference from laser toning is usually noticed from the 3rd or 4th session. A visitor on a one-week trip will not complete a course in Seoul. Treat a single session as exactly that, and ask at the remote consultation whether it makes sense for your skin.
- **Longer stays and repeat visitors.** If you are in Seoul for several weeks, or come back regularly, the weekly and two-weekly schedules above become realistic. Tell the clinic your dates so the doctor can plan around them.
- **If a pigment laser is added, plan for scabs.** With Clarity II or Lucas, allow for 3–7 days in which treated spots may darken or form fine scabs. Put that session early in your trip, or leave it for a visit when you have the time.
- **Sun is the real scheduling issue.** The clinic advises thorough sun protection for at least 2–4 weeks after any laser treatment and suggests avoiding laser treatment in periods with a lot of outdoor activity. If the rest of your holiday is beaches or hiking, say so.

## Aftercare

- **Sunscreen.** After laser treatment the skin is more sensitive and the risk of pigmentation from UV exposure rises. Apply SPF 50+ sunscreen thoroughly for at least 2–4 weeks and use a hat or parasol.
- **Temporary darkening.** With pigment lasers, temporary post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation can occur; the clinic says this improves naturally within 2–4 weeks and can be prevented with thorough sun protection.
- **Pregnancy and breastfeeding.** Laser treatments are not recommended; the clinic advises waiting until both are complete.
- **After you fly home.** Send photos through your messenger or the live chat and the clinic reviews your progress. A written aftercare guide is provided in English, Japanese and Chinese.

## FAQ

### How many laser toning sessions do I need?

The clinic recommends 10 or more sessions of laser toning in total, with a difference usually noticed from the 3rd or 4th session, and 5–10 sessions of Ulblanc. Its skin-tone protocols run from 5 sessions at 2-week intervals for mild concerns to 15 or more for severe ones. Your own number is set at the consultation.

### Is there downtime after laser toning?

The clinic describes laser toning as having almost no downtime, with an immediate return to daily activities; the same is stated for Ulblanc. If Clarity II or Lucas is added for pigment, allow 3–7 days in which fine scabs or darkening may appear.

### How much does laser toning cost at LIV?

The skin-tone page lists toning at KRW 150,000~ for a single session, excluding VAT. The final price is determined after consultation. Foreign patients pay from the same price list as Korean patients.

### Is one session during a trip enough?

It depends on what you expect. The treatment works gradually over repeated sessions at weekly or two-weekly intervals, so a single session is one step rather than a full course. The doctor can tell you at the consultation what one session can reasonably do for your skin.

### Do I need to avoid the sun afterwards?

Yes. The clinic asks for SPF 50+ sunscreen for at least 2–4 weeks after laser treatment, plus a hat or parasol, because treated skin is more prone to pigmentation from UV exposure.
